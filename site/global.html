For internationals around Konkuk

New to Seoul, or just new to this neighborhood? Mingle is an unmanned dessert café in Hwayang-dong that never closes — a calm place to study at 2 a.m., grab a boxed dessert on your way home, or meet a language partner over coffee. No staff, no pressure, no small talk unless you want it.

Korean Table and Global Dessert Night are getting ready to launch. Until then, say hi on the neighborhood board — Korean or English, both are welcome.
