How to use a self-service cafe

There is no staff on site most of the time. Nothing here needs Korean — but here is exactly what happens, step by step.

1

Walk in

The door is open 24 hours. No code, no reception.

2

Pick your dessert

Cakes and puddings are in the fridge. Cookies and baked goods are on the shelves.

3

Order drinks at the kiosk

The screen has an English option. Cards and mobile payment work; some overseas-issued cards may be declined depending on the issuer.

4

Sit as long as you like

No time limit. Power outlets are available at most seats.

5

Clear your table

Separate cups and packaging into the bins near the exit.

Address

  • Address10-1 Hwayang-dong, Gwangjin-gu, Seoul
    39, Neungdong-ro 13-gil
  • SubwayKonkuk Univ. Station (Line 2 · 7), walking distance
  • Hours24 hours, every day

If something breaks

Post on the community board and we will see it. For emergencies call 112 (police) or 119 (fire and ambulance).
