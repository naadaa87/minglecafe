What Mingle is

A place, not just a chatroom

Most ways to meet people in a new city stay online. Mingle starts from a real room you can walk into. You post here, and then you actually sit down together — at a table we can point you to.

Study & work together

Language exams, side projects, thesis writing. Find people going through the same thing this month.

Korean & English tables

Half Korean, half English, no pressure. Mistakes are the point.

Neighbourhood board

Ask anything about living around Konkuk — clinics, phone plans, where to fix a laptop.

Safety first

House rules, in short

  • Members must be 19 or older.
  • First meetings happen at Mingle or another public place we have agreed on.
  • No dating or sexual purposes, no sales, no religion, no investment pitches.
  • Do not post phone numbers or messenger IDs. Talk inside Mingle.
  • Never send money to someone you met here. Report it instead.
  • In an emergency, call 112 (police) or 119 (fire and ambulance).
