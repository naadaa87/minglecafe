Opening a Mingle

Mingle is a self-service dessert cafe brand operated by Blank Inc. This page is a short English summary; the full Korean guide has the numbers, the store models and the required legal disclosures.

What the brand is

A 24-hour unmanned cafe built around desserts rather than coffee alone, with enough room for people to stay. The operating model is designed so one visit a day is enough to keep the store running.

What we do not promise

We do not publish expected revenue figures. Sales depend on the location, the rent, the season and how the store is run. Any numbers you see elsewhere should be treated as one store's record, not a forecast for yours.
